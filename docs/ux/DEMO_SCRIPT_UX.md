# 3-minute demo (what is built today)

1. Open the public page, scroll to "Run the whole trade loop": watch the six-step orbit story, press Pause.
2. Switch language to Tamil, then Chinese. Toggle dark mode (brand green). Settings > Appearance shows "Match my device".
3. Sign in (demo). Home: "Needs you" strip, then the live Trade Orbit. Tab to a node, press Enter for its numbers.
4. Deals > Sales Order SO-2026-0001. "Supplier side": the profit ring (customer price against supplier cost), the stamps (Roadmap ones are dashed and labelled).
5. Press Compare: three supplier quotes ranked with the reason in words. Pick a supplier, "Draft purchase order from the best quote": the draft joins the same deal; the owner approves before it goes out.
6. Deals > an order: issue the proforma, record the deposit (receipt is made), create the delivery order and copy its link, sign it on the public page `#/d/<token>`, issue the invoice, record the balance. Open Audit log.
