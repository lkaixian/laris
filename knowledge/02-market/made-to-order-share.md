# Made-to-order / quote-driven share of SMEs

**Direct figure: none found.** No DOSM, SME Corp or MDEC publication located reports the share of Malaysian SMEs that price per job. Do not quote any percentage for this.

## Closest proxies (all from [[msme-count-dosm-ec2023]])
- Construction 69,015 MSMEs (6.5%) plus manufacturing 51,481 (4.8%) = 120,496 (11.3%, derived sum). Upper-bound for job-priced trades only if every one were made-to-order, which is not true of manufacturing in general.
- Services 936,793 (87.6%) is too coarse: it mixes retail/F&B (not quoted) with printing, signage, tailoring, catering, repair and trade services (quoted). Split needs [[msme-wholesale-retail-fnb-count]] and MSIC class-level data (REAL DATA needed).
- Micro firms dominate ([[msme-count-by-size-2023]]: 69.7% in 2023; over 77% in 2025 per [[msme-count-2025-minister]]), which suggests owner-run, WhatsApp-quoted jobs, but this is inference.
