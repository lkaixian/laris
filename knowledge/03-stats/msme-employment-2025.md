# MSME employment 2025

- value: 8.09 million persons = 48.7% of national employment (2024: 7.97 million, 48.5%); growth 1.5%
- unit: persons / %
- year: 2025
- Value added per worker RM85,299 (+4.1%).
- Caveat: DOSM's 2024 release stated 2024 employment as 8.10 million; the 2025 release shows 7.97 million for 2024 (revision). See [[msme-gdp-share-2025]].
