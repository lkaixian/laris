# MSME share of GDP 2025

- value: 39.7 (% of GDP); value added RM689.8 billion; growth 5.7% vs national 5.2%
- unit: % / RM billion
- year: 2025
- Sector split of MSME value added: services + manufacturing combined 84.5%; agriculture 8.4%; construction 5.3%; mining and quarrying 0.5%.
- Source: DOSM MSME Performance 2025 (released 2026-07-30). See [[msme-employment-2025]], [[msme-exports-2025]].
