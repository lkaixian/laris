# e-Invoice (MyInvois) as of Oct 2026

Related: [[pdpa-malaysia]], [[sst-and-quotation-docs]]

## FINDING THAT CHANGES THE BRIEF
The brief assumed "RM1M exemption, RM1-5M live 1 Jan 2026". This is **superseded**: after the PM's 30 Aug 2026 National Day address, LHDN raised the exemption threshold **RM1M -> RM3M, effective 1 Sep 2026** (over 1.1 million businesses exempt; voluntary use encouraged). Carried in e-Invoice Guideline v4.8; exemption is not automatic in every case (e.g. a non-individual shareholder / group above a turnover level loses it).
Most LARIS target SMEs (under RM3M annual sales) are therefore **not mandated**. e-Invoice is an optional upsell, not a compliance driver.
