# WhatsApp Business app

Official WhatsApp FAQ fetch failed (404); facts are from vendor blogs (Trengo, Qiscus, ChatDaddy), not WhatsApp itself.

- Catalog up to ~500 items with prices and photos; native catalog has no checkout or inventory sync.
- Quick replies via "/" (cap reported as 50). Labels reported cap 20, private to the business. Greeting and away messages.
- SleekFlow reports the catalog supports Malay, Mandarin and English (https://sleekflow.io/blog/malaysia-whatsapp-catalogue.md).
- No supplier-quote ledger, cost book, margin logic or automated follow-up; labels and quick replies are manual.
