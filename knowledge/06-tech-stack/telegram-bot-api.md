# ADR: Telegram Bot API as demo/fallback channel

Related: [[whatsapp-cloud-api]], [[pricing-numbers]]

- Free; no per-message charge. Limits: about 1 msg/s per chat, 20 msg/min in groups, about 30 msg/s bulk (429 beyond). Paid broadcasts (up to 1,000 msg/s, 0.1 Star each) need 100,000 Stars and 100,000 MAU, irrelevant to LARIS.
- Files: bot download max 20 MB (getFile), upload max 50 MB. Voice notes fit.
- Cost per quote conversation: RM0. Good for hackathon demo. Real SME customer adoption of Telegram vs WhatsApp: REAL DATA needed.
