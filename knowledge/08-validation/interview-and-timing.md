# Interview script and timing log

Rules: consent first; anonymise; no real customer/company records enter the app (synthetic only).

## Interview (2-3 owners + Yb's shop)
1. What trade? How many staff? How many quotes a week?
2. Walk me through your last quote, from enquiry to sent. Where did the price come from?
3. When did a supplier last raise a price? How did you find out? Did you re-quote?
4. Where do supplier quotations live (WhatsApp, email, paper)? How do you find them again?
5. How many quotes got no reply? Who chases?
6. Would you pay per approved quote? How much?
