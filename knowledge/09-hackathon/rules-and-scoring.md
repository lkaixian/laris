# Rules and scoring map

**Deadline:** Sun 25 Oct 2026, 7:59:59 AM (handbook; FAQ says 11:59 PM, follow handbook). Submit Sat night.
**Hard rules:** Replit compulsory (proof in declaration); RunPod for any GPU work (screenshot Pod ID/endpoint, GPU, runtime); video 5-7 min (-1 per 30 s over 7:00); official template repo; no API keys in repo; declare every AI tool/model/dataset; synthetic data only; sponsor checkbox needs Replit + at least one RunPod model call.
**Loses marks:** re-skinned chatbot; copying Orbyte HR/admin features; real payments/tax filings/legal advice; real customer records; no PDPA explanation.

## 5-step journey -> LARIS
1. Trigger: supplier quotation/receipt arrives, or customer enquiry arrives
2. Data: cost book, price list/templates, job queue, client history
3. Decision: AI extracts with confidence; rules price; margin guard
4. Approval: owner approves; discounts above limit need owner
5. Outcome + log: quote PDF sent, tracked, 48h follow-up, audit log
