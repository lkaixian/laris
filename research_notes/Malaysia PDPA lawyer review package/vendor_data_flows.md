### Inferences
- LARIS (the business using the SaaS, or LARIS as processor for its tenants) is the party that decides purposes; vendors are processors (Replit, RunPod, Mailtrap, Meta, per their own pages). Telegram and ILMU do not describe themselves that way in what I found. [S]
- PDPA s.129 note (not from vendor pages, from my general knowledge, [S], not verified against the statute today): s.129 restricts transfer of personal data outside Malaysia unless a listed condition applies; the 2024 amendment (Act A1727) replaced the Minister's whitelist with tests such as a destination with substantially similar law or adequate protection, or consent/contract/other listed grounds. A lawyer should confirm current text and any guidelines. On the facts above: ILMU (Malaysia only, per its docs) is the only vendor not showing a cross-border element. Replit, RunPod, Mailtrap, Meta and Telegram involve or may involve processing outside Malaysia (US confirmed for Mailtrap, RunPod policy, Replit subprocessors; others unspecified), so each is a transfer-question item. Offered SCCs are EU/UK/Swiss instruments, not Malaysian ones. Vendors' DPAs do not mention Malaysia (Replit, Mailtrap confirmed; RunPod and Meta not seen).
- RunPod's 30-minute result retention means "audio/images discarded" in LARIS docs describes LARIS storage only, not the vendor side.
